Personal training

Your personal trainer designs a tailored plan based on where you're starting from and what you want to achieve. They're with you every session so you train with good technique and safely, adjust the plan as you progress and give you a personalised routine so you can also train outside the centre.

Ideal if you want to

  • Build strength and muscle tone
  • Lose weight healthily
  • Improve your posture
  • Look after your long-term health
  • Prepare for a sports challenge
  • Get back into training after a break
Ask for information
02

Rehabilitation

We don't do conventional rehab: we specialise in a much more effective process, based on muscle work and guided exercise. Exercise-based physiotherapists and return-to-sport trainers stay with you from the first assessment until you're safely back to your activity, and help you avoid getting injured again.

We can help with

  • Back and neck pain
  • Sports injuries
  • Return to sport after injury
  • Post-surgery recovery
  • Joint discomfort
  • Getting back to your sport safely

FAQ

Your questions answered

Where is Activa'M?

We're at Carrer de Malats, 22, in the Sant Andreu neighbourhood of Barcelona.

What are your opening hours?

We're open from 7 am to 10 pm, always by appointment booked in advance on WhatsApp.

How do I book?

Message us on WhatsApp at +34 626 660 770. We'll ask what you need and suggest a day and time.

How is your rehabilitation different from conventional physiotherapy?

We work on recovery through exercise and muscle work. Exercise-based physiotherapists and return-to-sport trainers stay with you until you're safely back to your activity.

Do I need to be fit to start?

No. Every plan is adapted to your starting point, age, injuries and goals, whether you come to train or to recover.

What should I bring to my first session?

Comfortable clothes to move in and, if you have an injury, any medical reports or scans. They help us assess your case.

Can I combine training, rehabilitation and nutrition?

Yes. That's one of the advantages of Activa'M: our professionals work as a team and coordinate your plan.

Do you run group classes?

Yes. Pilates, toning and strength in small groups at Activa'M Pilates. All the details are at activampilates.cat.

Do you speak English?

Yes, just message us on WhatsApp in English and we'll take it from there.
